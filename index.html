The week

Counted out. Back in the chair.

On Monday 23 November 2026, NVIDIA said Jensen Huang was reinstated as chief executive and a member of the board. He had resigned earlier that month. The absence was about eleven or twelve days. Outlets including Decrypt, Inc., Business Insider, and The Straits Times covered the reversal the same day and the next.

The board's public line, as reported, was that he was the right person to lead the company. An investigation tied to the exit was still described as open. This page does not retry the allegations. The story it keeps is the return.

The stock moved hard that day. Business Insider reported NVIDIA up about 31 percent by the close. The internet did not wait for the close. The memes were already up.
